Push-Up Counter Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-10

About this app

Push-Up Counter is an offline exercise counter and optional screen-time tool for adults. You do not create an account. The app has no ads, purchases, subscriptions or developer analytics.

Camera and exercise data

With your consent and Android camera permission, live camera frames are processed on this device to estimate body position and count repetitions or plank time. Frames and pose points are used in memory and are not recorded, uploaded or retained as photos or video. The camera is used only for an active workout screen. Workout totals, daily history, earned time and session-save bookkeeping are stored privately on this device.

Installed apps and optional app limits

After the app-limits disclosure and your agreement, the app reads the names and package IDs of launchable apps to show an app picker. Only your chosen package IDs are saved. If you enable monitoring and grant Usage Access, display-over-apps access and notifications, the app reads foreground-app usage events while it runs, including when its own screen is closed. These events are processed in memory to consume earned time and display an overlay on a selected app when time runs out. It does not save a separate usage or browsing history, read screen contents, messages or contacts, or upload app lists.

Control over monitoring

App limits are optional. A persistent notification identifies active monitoring and provides Stop. You can also pause from Train or the blocker. Monitoring does not consume time while the screen is off or locked. Enabled monitoring can resume after a device restart, subject to Android restrictions, only with the required consent and permissions. System settings, home screens, calling apps and the default messaging app are excluded from limits. Reset app-limit permissions in Settings stops monitoring, removes selections and resets in-app consent; revoke Android access grants in system settings.

Spoken counts

If enabled, the count number is passed to your installed Android text-to-speech engine. The app selects only a voice reported as installed and not requiring a network connection. If no suitable offline voice is available, visual counting continues without speech. Camera frames, app lists and saved history are not passed to the speech engine. The speech engine is separately supplied by Android or another provider and has its own settings. You can disable Voice count in Settings or during the camera disclosure.

Sharing and network access

The app does not request Internet permission and does not transmit camera data, workout records, app inventory or usage events to the developer or advertising services. AndroidX/CameraX, LiteRT and the bundled MoveNet model support local interface, camera and inference functions. Optional speech uses the separately installed engine as described above. Opening a policy link launches your browser; browser and hosting-provider practices apply to that visit. No advertising or analytics scripts are included in the supplied policy page.

Retention, deletion and security

App settings, selected package IDs, workout history, earned time and session-save bookkeeping remain in Android private app storage until removed. Clear workout history removes the displayed daily workout records and keeps earned time and settings. Reset app-limit permissions removes selected app IDs and in-app consent. To delete all local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Push-Up Counter > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app. The app disables Android cloud backup and device transfer. Device security and any separately installed speech engine remain subject to Android and that provider. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete records stored only on your phone.

Children and medical use

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and over. The app is not designed for children or for parental surveillance. It provides general fitness estimates, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. For medical questions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Policy changes

A future release may update this policy. The updated date above identifies this version. Material changes to sensitive-data access require updated disclosure and consent where applicable.
